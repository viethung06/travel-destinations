Top 3 Destinations

1. Paris, France

Paris is famous for its architecture, museums, food, and romantic atmosphere. The Eiffel Tower and the Louvre are among its most popular attractions.

2. Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo combines modern technology with traditional Japanese culture. Visitors can explore busy streets, temples, restaurants, and unique neighborhoods.

3. Bali, Indonesia

Bali is known for tropical beaches, temples, rice terraces, and beautiful natural scenery. It is a great destination for relaxation and exploration.

Why I Like These Destinations

  • They offer different cultures and experiences.
  • They have famous attractions and beautiful scenery.
  • They provide many opportunities for photography and exploration.
